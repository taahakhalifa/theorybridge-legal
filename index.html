Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Theory Bridge ("the app", "we", "us") is a UK driving theory comprehension app designed for adult learners. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

We believe in keeping things simple and transparent — just like our approach to learning.

What data do we collect?

We collect only the data needed to provide your personalised learning experience:

DataWhy we need it
Email addressTo create and secure your account
Learning preferencesYour language, confidence level, and topic preferences (set during onboarding) to tailor your experience
Study progressTopics studied, questions answered, scores, and accuracy — so we can track your improvement
Vocabulary masteryWhich keywords you have learned, are learning, or still need to review
Mistake recordsQuestions you got wrong, so we can help you review and retry them using spaced repetition
Mock test resultsYour mock test scores and topic breakdowns, so you can see how close you are to passing
Daily streakThe date you last used the app, to calculate your study streak

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data beyond what is listed above.

How do we use your data?

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide your personalised learning experience
  • Track your study progress and identify weak areas
  • Power features like spaced repetition, vocabulary mastery, and mock test history
  • Show you meaningful statistics (accuracy, streak, improvement over time)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your learning.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which runs on AWS infrastructure. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our database uses Row Level Security, meaning each user can only access their own data.

We take appropriate technical measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

Third-party sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties. Full stop.

  • No advertising networks
  • No analytics SDKs or third-party tracking
  • No data brokers
  • No social media integrations

Your data stays between you and Theory Bridge.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you stop using the app, your data remains available should you return.

If you delete your account, we will delete all your personal data within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access your data — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct your data — ask us to fix any inaccuracies
  • Delete your data — request full deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Restrict processing — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Data portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Object — object to any processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the Settings screen within the app. Upon successful completion of the deletion request, all your progress, answers, vocabulary, and mock test data will be removed. If the deletion request fails, you will be notified and your data will remain until the issue is resolved.

Alternatively, you can request deletion by emailing:

support@theorybridge.app

We will process email deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

Theory Bridge is designed for adult learners aged 17 and over who are preparing for the UK driving theory test. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 17. If we become aware that a child under 17 has created an account, we will delete that account promptly.

Cookies and device storage

The app does not use cookies. We use minimal secure device storage on your device for app functionality (such as authentication tokens). No tracking cookies or third-party cookies are used.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through an in-app notification. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at:

support@theorybridge.app

This privacy policy is written in plain English to be accessible to all our users, consistent with Theory Bridge's mission of making complex information easier to understand.
