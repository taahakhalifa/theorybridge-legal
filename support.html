Support — Theory Bridge

For help with the app, email support@theorybridge.co.uk.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data in the app, from the Settings screen. You can also email support@theorybridge.co.uk, and we'll act on your request within 30 days.

Purchases

Theory Bridge Pro is bought through Apple or Google. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple or Google account settings. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

See the privacy policy for what data the app collects and how long it's kept.
